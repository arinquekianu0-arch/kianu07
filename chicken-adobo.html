Filipino Chicken Adobo

Home | Pancakes | Chicken Adobo | Spaghetti

Description

Chicken adobo is a popular Filipino dish cooked with soy sauce, vinegar, garlic, and spices.

Ingredients

  • 1 kilogram chicken
  • 1/2 cup soy sauce
  • 1/2 cup vinegar
  • 1 cup water
  • 5 cloves garlic, crushed
  • 2 bay leaves
  • 1 teaspoon whole peppercorns

Instructions

  1. Place the chicken, soy sauce, and garlic in a bowl. Mix and let it sit for 15 minutes.
  2. Put the chicken mixture in a pot.
  3. Add vinegar, water, bay leaves, and peppercorns.
  4. Bring to a boil, then lower the heat.
  5. Cover and simmer until the chicken is fully cooked and tender.
  6. Serve with rice.
