Easy Pancakes

Home | Pancakes | Chicken Adobo | Spaghetti

Description

Easy pancakes are soft, fluffy, and perfect for breakfast.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup all-purpose flour
  • 1 tablespoon sugar
  • 1 teaspoon baking powder
  • 1 egg
  • 3/4 cup milk
  • 2 tablespoons melted butter

Instructions

  1. Mix the flour, sugar, and baking powder in a bowl.
  2. Add the egg, milk, and melted butter.
  3. Mix until combined. Do not overmix.
  4. Pour some batter into a lightly greased pan.
  5. Cook until bubbles appear, then flip and cook the other side.
  6. Serve warm with your favorite topping.
