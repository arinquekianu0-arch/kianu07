Simple Spaghetti

Home | Pancakes | Chicken Adobo | Spaghetti

Description

Simple spaghetti is an easy pasta meal made with tomato sauce and ground meat.

Ingredients

  • 250 grams spaghetti
  • 200 grams ground meat
  • 1 small onion, chopped
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 cup tomato sauce
  • 1 tablespoon cooking oil
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • Grated cheese (optional)

Instructions

  1. Cook the spaghetti according to the package instructions.
  2. Heat oil in a pan. Cook the onion and garlic until soft.
  3. Add the ground meat and cook until no longer pink.
  4. Add the tomato sauce, salt, and pepper.
  5. Simmer the sauce for about 10 minutes.
  6. Serve the sauce over the cooked spaghetti and add cheese if desired.
